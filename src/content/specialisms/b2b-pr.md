B2B buyers do their research before they ever speak to a salesperson — and increasingly, that research starts with an AI engine, not a search results page. If your expertise isn't visible and citable at the point someone is forming a shortlist, a competitor's will be instead.

B2B audiences are also more sceptical of generic marketing claims than consumer audiences tend to be. Credibility has to be earned through genuine expertise and third-party validation — real commentary on real industry challenges, not recycled announcements.

## Sectors this spans

B2B experience runs deepest in **B2B technology** — including taking companies into new markets from a standing start — and **professional services**, where reputation is the entire product being sold. It also covers the corporate and investor-facing side of **property & placemaking**, and B2B relationships within construction.

## What this looks like in practice

- Thought leadership built on genuine points of view, not recycled trend commentary
- Trade press and vertical media relationships in the publications your buyers actually read
- Award submissions and accreditation that build third-party proof of credibility
- Content and structured data built so AI engines can accurately understand and cite what your business does
