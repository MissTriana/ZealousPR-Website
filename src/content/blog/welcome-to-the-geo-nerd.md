For sixteen years, my job has been the same at its core: help a business earn trust with the people who matter to it. What's changed is who's doing the listening.

A growing share of the audience for your PR isn't a journalist, and it isn't a person scrolling a results page — it's an AI engine, reading the web on someone else's behalf and handing back a single answer. That's not a threat to PR. It's the same job, with a new gatekeeper.

This is where we'll be writing about it: what's working, what's hype, and what a genuinely good PR & GEO strategy looks like in practice. Glad you're here.
